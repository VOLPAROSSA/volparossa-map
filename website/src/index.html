How to read a report

Recent reports apply to the indicated direction and map version. Expired, missing, sparse or conflicting reports leave conditions unknown. A signature identifies a publisher. Speed and independent corroboration need separate evidence.

{{shield_check}} Reading updates and contributing observations are separate choices. You can stop contributing at any time.
